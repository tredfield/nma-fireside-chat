Fireside Chat Outline: NMA 
1. Opening: who NMA is (≈2 min) 
* NMA is a volunteer, non-profit organization based in Washington State. 
* It was formed in 1937 to bring Washington's motorcycle clubs together. In 1973, as riding areas came under pressure, it shifted its focus to public land access rights for off-road riders. 
* NMA's reps lobby in Olympia and sit on land planning sessions, committees, and grant funding committees.
* NMA’s goal is make Washington the best place to ride, teach stewardship and etiquette, and build positive relationships with land managers and other trail users.
2. GPNF: 2026 was a big year there
The Trans-Cascadia partnership (≈2.5 min)
* NMA and Trans-Cascadia began formal talks in fall 2025, and a $140k agreement was signed in April 2026.
* Results: 64.31 miles of high-use trail were worked in the Cowlitz Valley and Mt. Adams Ranger Districts. The work covered drainage, tread repair, corridor clearing, logout, and erosion control, all to USFS standards.
* Volunteers: 2,840+ documented hours across two work-party weekends, with about 300 days of Trans-Cascadia pro crew working the trails.
* Collaboration: dirt bikers and mountain bikers worked side by side. NMA believes it is important to collaborate with and form relationships with other user groups. It makes the trails better for everyone.
The hand crew: tread untouched for 30 years (≈1.5 min)
* A two-person hand crew restored tread that hadn't been touched in about 30 years, including drainage, switchbacks, out-slope, whoops, and roots, finished with lots of raking.
* Done: Silver Creek #258, Allen Mountain #269, Greenwood Lake #253, Big Creek #252, and Cave Creek #255.
* In progress: Osbourne #250.
* Three regular volunteers helped the pro crew during their work.
4. Beyond the work: the riding side (≈30 sec) (new)
* NMA also runs a competition program with Enduro, Off-Road, and Trials series, plus Family Fun Runs.
* As a result, the NMA has produced several athletes throughout the years who have competed on the national and international levels. All of our events are family orientated where it’s not unusual to find families with multiple members competing and and participating.
5. Behind the scenes (≈1 min) (new)
* Membership dues and donations support:
   * the Moto-Volunteer Trail Crew
   * Heavy Maintenance Crew support and administration
   * land use and legislative projects
   * competition and poker run schedules
* Grants (NOVA and RTP) reimburse expenses, so every membership and donor dollar gets "recycled" into more trail work.
6. Close: how to get involved (≈30 sec)
* Volunteer at a work party, since no experience is needed and you can earn a free membership and other rewards for your hard work.
* Join as a member, donate, or sponsor.
