<div class="content">
  <h2>Who NMA is</h2>
  <ul class="points">
    <li>NMA is a volunteer, non-profit organization based in Washington State.</li>
    <li>It was formed in <strong>1937</strong> to bring Washington's motorcycle clubs together. In <strong>1973</strong>, as riding areas came under pressure, it shifted its focus to public land access rights for off-road riders.</li>
    <li>NMA's reps lobby in Olympia and sit on land planning sessions, committees, and grant funding committees.</li>
    <li>NMA's goal is to make Washington the best place to ride, teach stewardship and etiquette, and build positive relationships with land managers and other trail users.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<!--

  <h2>The Trans-Cascadia partnership</h2>
  <ul class="points">
    <li>NMA and Trans-Cascadia began formal talks in fall 2025, and a <strong>$140k agreement</strong> was signed in April 2026.</li>
    <li><strong>Results:</strong> 64.31 miles of high-use trail were worked in the Cowlitz Valley and Mt. Adams Ranger Districts. The work covered drainage, tread repair, corridor clearing, logout, and erosion control, all to USFS standards.</li>
  </ul>
